1. Dashboard Overview
The PREC Dashboard is a simple content management and property administration system designed to
help the PREC team manage website content, property listings, inquiries, and general company information
without requiring technical expertise.
The system should prioritize:
Simplicity
Ease of navigation
Accessibility
Mobile responsiveness
Quick content management
Clear data organization
The dashboard will be accessible only to authorized PREC staff through a secure login system.

13. Summary
The PREC Dashboard/CMS is intended to provide a simple and efficient internal management system for
handling:
Property listings
Website content
Customer inquiries
Featured properties
Company updates
Website settings
Basic analytics
The platform should prioritize ease of use, fast content updates, and accessibility while maintaining a
professional and modern administrative experience for the PREC team.
